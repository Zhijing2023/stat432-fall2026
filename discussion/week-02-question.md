question:
The more predictors are added, the more fitted the model will be to the data. However, the model may have experience such as overfitted problems. Under those conditions, how can one balance between more information and more uncertainty?

answer:
With a larger sample size, the cost of estimating each additional parameter becomes smaller.Its appropriateness depends partly on how much data supports it.
